I am a PhD student in Computer Science at The University of Texas at Austin, advised by Prof. Aditya Akella. I work on systems research with a focus on how to apply ML to systems problems. I am involved with the LDOS venture.

I previously completed my Master's degree in Computer Science & Engineering at University of Michigan, Ann Arbor, where I worked with Profs Ryan Huang and Mosharaf Chowdhury on fault tolerance and AI resource management. I was recognized with the Moeller Award for outstanding academic achievements and social responsibility.

I was also an intern at the Max Planck Institute for Software Systems(MPI-SWS) during Summer 2025, where I worked with Profs Reto Achermann and Andrea Lattuada on x86 hardware modeling for paging.

Previously, I worked as a Research Engineer at the National University of Singapore in the ECE department under Prof Mohan Guruswamy on anomaly detection in Software Defined Networking. I also have industry experience as a Software Engineer at Google and a Quantitative Research Intern at JP Morgan Chase & Co.

I graduated with a B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering from Indian Institute of Technology Madras in 2022. For more details, check my projects, CV, or reach out via email.
